# Archived collections

These days were collected before six basket items were re-pinned to different
products on 2026-09-29. They are kept because they are valid observations of the
products named in them — they are simply not comparable with what came after.

The index takes its base from the first day in `data/prices/`, and a base
holding the old products would have compared a new product's price against an
old one's for those six items, forever. Four days of history was the cheaper
thing to discard.

Migros's online catalogue carries no standard somun: only artisan, organic and
packaged specialty breads. Pinning one would have meant tracking a premium
product and calling it bread, so bread is now collected from ŞOK alone.

**Known gap.** There is no substitution handling in the index. A product that is
delisted simply drops out, which is fine, but a deliberate re-pin like this one
corrupts the base. Proper CPI practice links the old and new series at the
substitution point; until that exists, re-pinning means resetting the base.
